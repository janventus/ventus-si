About us

At Ventus d.o.o., we have been servicing and maintaining cooling, heating and ventilation equipment and systems in commercial buildings across Slovenia since 2012.

We work on chillers, heat pumps, air handling and ventilation units, fan coil units, fire and smoke dampers, and split systems in server and technical rooms. We carry out regular inspections and maintenance, leak checks and refrigerant top-ups, fault diagnostics and repairs, and installation and commissioning of equipment.

Our customers are companies, building owners and facility managers. We are an authorised service partner for Clivet and Daikin and are entered in the Slovenian register of authorised companies for refrigeration, air conditioning and heat pumps (no. 293).

  • Refrigerant leak checksPeriodic checks with a certificate for equipment that F-⁠gas legislation requires to be checked.
  • F-gas recordsWe keep F-⁠gas records for our customers and help with the maintenance logbook.
  • Installation and commissioningSupply, installation and first start-up of cooling, heating and ventilation equipment.
