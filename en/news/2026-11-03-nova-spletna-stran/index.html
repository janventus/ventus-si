Ventus has a new website

Published 3 November 2026

The redesigned ventus.si brings services grouped by equipment type, a summary of F-gas legislation and a contact form.

Ventus d.o.o. has a new website. We built it so that you can quickly see which equipment we service and maintain, and how to get in touch with us.

What you will find

  • Services by equipment type. Each group has its own page: chillers, heat pumps, air handling and ventilation units, fan coil units, fire and smoke dampers, F-gases and refrigerants, and server and technical rooms. All of them are listed on the Services page.
  • Recommended intervals. The service pages show recommended intervals for inspections and servicing.
  • F-gas legislation. The F-gases and refrigerants page summarises the obligations of equipment operators.
  • Contact form. You can send us an enquiry or a question directly from the website.
  • Slovenian and English. All content is available in both languages.

About us

Ventus services and maintains heating, cooling and ventilation equipment and systems in commercial buildings across Slovenia. Authorised service partner: Clivet and Daikin.

Get in touch

E-mail us at info@ventus.si, call +386 51 358 100 or use the contact form.
