HVAC servicing and maintenance

We service and maintain cooling, heating and ventilation equipment and systems in commercial buildings across Slovenia. Choose a piece of equipment on the building cross-section or find the service in the list.

List of services

  1. 1ChillersPreventive inspections, seasonal start-up and shutdown, and refrigerant leak checks.
  2. 2Heat pumpsServicing, performance and leak checks, and changeover between heating and cooling.
  3. 3AHUs and ventilation unitsInspection and maintenance of the unit and its controls.
  4. 4Fan coil unitsFilter and heat exchanger cleaning, checks of unit operation and its controls, and condensate drain checks.
  5. 5Fire and smoke dampersServicing and inspections of fire and smoke dampers and smoke extraction fans.
  6. 6F-gases and refrigerantsF-⁠gas leak checks, refrigerant top-ups, certificates and F-⁠gas records.
  7. 7Server and technical roomsSplit system servicing in server, telecom and electrical rooms.

For all equipment

Services we provide for every type of equipment and system.

  • Fault diagnostics and repairs

    When equipment reports a fault or does not work correctly, we find the cause and fix it. Where needed, we replace worn parts.

  • Installation and commissioning

    We advise on new cooling, heating and ventilation equipment and take care of supply, installation and first start-up – also when replacing ageing equipment or extending an existing system.

  • Seasonal start-up and shutdown

    At the start and end of each season we start up or shut down the system correctly, including the changeover between cooling and heating.

  • Heat exchanger cleaning and disinfection

    We clean heat exchanger fins and disinfect the heat exchangers where needed.

  • Maintenance contracts

    Planned regular maintenance of the systems in your building, following the manufacturers’ instructions.

What the law requires

We work according to good professional practice and the applicable legislation. For operators of cooling equipment and heat pumps containing fluorinated greenhouse gases (F-⁠gases), the rules are:

  1. 1.refrigerant leak checks are mandatory for equipment containing 5 tonnes of CO₂ equivalent or more of F-⁠gases; how often depends on the charge: from 5 to less than 50 tonnes at least every 12 months, from 50 to less than 500 tonnes every 6 months, 500 tonnes or more every 3 months (Regulation (EU) 2024/573, Article 5)
  2. 2.with a leak detection system installed, the intervals are extended to 24, 12 and 6 months
  3. 3.registering equipment: the operator must register stationary equipment containing 3 kg or more of F-⁠gases with the ministry responsible for the environment no later than three months after installation (Slovenian decree on F-⁠gases, Article 4)

5 tonnes of CO₂ equivalent is, for example, approximately 2.40 kg of R410A or 7.41 kg of R32 (GWP values from Annex I of Regulation (EU) 2024/573).

Recommendation: regardless of the legal leak checks, we recommend having the equipment serviced by a qualified technician at least once a year.
