Fan coil unit servicing and cleaning

AI illustrationAI

Fan coil unit servicing covers cleaning or replacing the filters, cleaning and disinfecting the heat exchanger, checking the operation of the unit and its controls, and checking the condensate drain. It is intended for companies, building owners and facility managers. We work across Slovenia.

What fan coil unit servicing includes

  • Maintenance and basic cleaning
  • Filter cleaning or replacement
  • Heat exchanger cleaning and disinfection
  • Checks of unit operation and its controls
  • Condensate drain check
  • Supply and replacement of faulty spare parts

Recommended interval

Preventive service while the units are running in heating mode.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to service fan coil units?

We recommend a preventive service at least once a year, while the units are running in heating mode.

Is the disinfection harmful to people in the room?

We disinfect the heat exchanger using a method that is safe for health.
