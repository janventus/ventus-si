Air handling unit and ventilation unit servicing

AI illustrationAI

AHU servicing covers inspection of the unit and its controls, filter checks and replacement, checks of belts and fans, heat exchanger cleaning, and inspection of the ductwork and grilles. It is intended for companies, building owners and facility managers with central ventilation systems. We service air handling units and ventilation units across Slovenia.

What AHU and ventilation unit servicing includes

  • Inspection of the unit and its controls
  • Air filter condition checks and filter replacement where needed
  • Checks of belts and of motor and fan operation
  • Heat exchanger cleaning and disinfection
  • Inspection of ductwork and air distribution
  • Inspection of intake and exhaust grilles
  • Fault diagnostics and repairs

Looking after the ventilation system between services

Between service visits we recommend monitoring how dirty the air filters are, the condition of the belts and how the fans are running. Once or twice a year, a technician inspects the unit and its controls and carries out the other checks on the list.

What the law requires

For ventilation units and systems in buildings covered by the Slovenian Rules on ventilation and air-conditioning of buildings, a regular inspection is required at least once a year unless the operating instructions specify otherwise. The findings are entered in an inspection log kept by the system operator.

Source: Rules on ventilation and air-conditioning of buildings (in Slovenian; Official Gazette of the RS, Nos. 42/02, 105/02, 110/02 – ZGO-1, 61/17 – GZ and 199/21 – GZ-1), Article 30; scope in Articles 1 and 2.

Recommended interval

Inspection of the unit and its controls; the air distribution system at least once a year.

Frequently asked questions

How often should a technician inspect an air handling unit?

We recommend once or twice a year. The building’s air distribution system should be inspected at least once a year.

What about rooms with flammable dust or grease vapour?

For systems in such rooms we recommend a routine check every three months, with cleaning where necessary.

Do you help keep the maintenance logbook?

Yes. We help you keep the logbook of operation, servicing and maintenance of the ventilation system.
